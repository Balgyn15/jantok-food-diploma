## Secondary observations (not user validation)

On 22 October 2026, public Astana service pages show that prepared-meal delivery and multi-day programs are already offered locally: [FIT MEAL](https://www.fitmeal.kz/) publishes a seven-day tariff and daily delivery windows; [Hemingway Healthy Club](https://hhc.kz/) displays durations up to 30 days, an itemized total and a claim to account for allergies/preferences; [Healthy Nation](https://healthy-nation.kz/) presents a weekly menu and meal-replacement action. These observations support the relevance of this service category and inform [the system comparison](SYSTEM_COMPARISON.md). They do **not** establish whether families need separate member profiles, whether advertised features work reliably, or whether a client has agreed to the proposed solution.

Recruit 3–5 representatives of the target audience (for example, households that plan meals for several people) or obtain a substantive agreement with the actual client. Ask for voluntary participation and avoid storing names, phone numbers, health details or recordings in Git. Use anonymous participant codes and keep consent records outside the repository.

Discussion prompts: current meal-planning process; difficulty managing different family restrictions; trust in recommendations; expectations for subscription price and change rules; what status updates are useful. Record date, participant role, observation or quote paraphrase, impact on requirement, and whether it was confirmed, contradicted or still assumed.
