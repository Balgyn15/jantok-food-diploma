# Project proposal (draft)

**Project:** JANTOK Food — personalized family meal subscription service for Astana.

**Team:** Yermakhanbet Balgyn Yerlanqyzy and Markhabat Ayana Markhabatkyzy (SE-2404). **Supervisor:** Omirzak Islam Amanzholuly.

## Problem and target users

The starting hypothesis is that households need one place to record dietary preferences and restrictions for each member, understand a recommended subscription and price, and track changes and fulfillment. Target users are households in Astana and operational staff. This is a hypothesis until user/client discussions are recorded under JANTOK-2.

## Aim and objectives

Aim: design and evaluate a safe, understandable subscription workflow for family meals. Objectives: (1) validate user and client needs; (2) compare research and existing systems; (3) specify testable requirements; (4) justify core technologies; (5) design architecture and key screens; (6) define a measurable evaluation plan.

## Preliminary scope

In scope: family account, individual nutrition questionnaire, menu recommendation, 30-day subscription, permitted menu changes, order/delivery status, and staff planning for one kitchen in Astana. Out of scope for this diploma milestone: expansion to other cities, courier app, marketplace, and live external-provider deployment. Any change requires a documented decision.

## Expected first-stage outcome

By 24 October 2026: evidence-backed problem statement, literature and system comparisons, requirements with acceptance criteria, context/use-case/component diagrams, prototype, evaluation plan, risks and milestone schedule. A full implementation is not required at this stage.

## Open validation points

- Confirm actual user pain points through 3–5 conversations or a substantive client agreement.
- Confirm nutrition and operational constraints with the client.
- Review scope and architecture with the supervisor; record actual feedback and resulting changes.
