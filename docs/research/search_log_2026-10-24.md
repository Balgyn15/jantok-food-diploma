## Selection and appraisal record

The preserved review selected recent scientific work with an explicit implication for meal selection, constraints, explanations, or evaluation. It retained trials with mixed or negative outcomes to prevent one-sided claims about personalization. Two official guidance sources were added for background and risk framing. The included set is **10 scientific publications (2022–2025) plus 2 official sources**, not 12 scientific papers.

| IDs | Reason retained | Appraisal limit recorded in the baseline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L01, L02, L06 | Reviews summarize approaches, evaluation gaps, and user involvement. | Their search criteria and heterogeneous included studies limit transfer. L01 was record/abstract-bounded. |
| L03, L04, L05 | Study choice, explanations, feedback, and acceptance in interactive food contexts. | App and deployment settings are small or context-specific; L03 was record/abstract-bounded. |
| L07, L08, L09 | Controlled studies give both positive and non-superior outcome examples. | Interventions and populations differ from a family meal service; L09 was record/abstract-bounded. |
| L10 | Model example for jointly representing food factors. | Dataset/model results do not validate user acceptance or allergy safety. |
| L11, L12 | Official general diet and food-allergy context. | Not empirical JANTOK user evidence and not Kazakhstan compliance guidance. |

The companion [research note](literature_review.md) maps each source to the proposed requirements and explains why the team cannot extrapolate medical benefit, market demand, or verified kitchen safety. The existing [review](../LITERATURE_REVIEW.md) contains the full bibliography and DOI/PubMed links.

## Reproducibility gaps and next logged update

The baseline cannot provide a screening-flow total because query strings, result counts, duplicate counts, language restrictions, exclusion reasons, and database export files were not retained. Avoid presenting it as a systematic review. A future update should record, at the time of each actual search: database and interface, date/time, exact query and filters, result count, deduplication, title/abstract and full-text decisions, versioned source links, and any changes to the synthesis. Searches performed after 24 October should be saved in a new dated file rather than backdated here.

Local evidence has a different provenance. Six anonymized interview paraphrases were supplied by the project owner for conversations dated 15 October; see [the participant supplement](../RELEVANCE_EVIDENCE_SUPPLEMENT.md). The interviews do not increase the scientific-source count. The five-service comparison uses public provider claims, not purchased or independently tested service behavior.
